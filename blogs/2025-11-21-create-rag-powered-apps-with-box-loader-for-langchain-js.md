I’m excited to announce the launch of langchainjs-box, a new document loader that brings the power of Box’s content management platform directly into your LangChain.js applications. This package makes it seamless to integrate your Box files and folders for building RAG applications. Powered by Box Markdown representations One of the most exciting features of this loader is its built-in support for Box’s new Markdown representations .
